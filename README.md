Each player ("hero") has 30 HP. Bring the opponent HP down to 0 to win

There are 3 kinds of cards:
- Creatures: Main attackers, denoted with stats. During combat, they attack an opponent in its lane if it exists. Otherwise, they attack the opponent hero
- Spells: Crowd control cards with no stats
- Terrain: Board modifiers with no stats. They can only be placed on the middle 3 lanes, and overwrite any previous terrain if there was one

The board has 5 lanes:
- The leftmost lane is "Heights", which some cards utilize in their description
- The middle 3 lanes are "Ground". Terrain may only be placed on these lanes
- The rightmost lane is "Water". Only "Amphibious" cards can be played here

Only one card can be placed in a lane unless it has "Team Up".\
Click on a card to show its description and abilities. Hover over any text in blue to give more info

Start of turn:
- Gain gold ("energy") equal to turn number
- Draw a card
- One player will go first ("initiative"), meaning the other player will go second ("reactive"). This is determined randomly on turn 1, then alternates each turn

Each turn has 4 phases:\
1 - Initiative player plays creatures only\
2 - Reactive player any cards\
3 - Initiative player plays spells and terrain only\
4 - Combat (Resolves lanewise, left to right)

When a hero is attacked: charge their block meter depending on how much the damage was:
- 1 damage: 1 charge
- 2 or 3 damage: 2 charge
- 4+ damage: 3 charge

When their meter reaches full, nullify all incoming damage and draw a unique card\
They can choose to either play it right then for free, or hold onto it

Every 2 turns, both players select a roguelike buff out of a list of 3, which will influence game state in various ways.\
Players have 1 reroll, which can be used to generate a new list for both players.

Buffs come from 6 unique classes:
- Elation (yellow)
- Misery (blue)
- Wrath (red)
- Fright (purple)
- Awe (pink)
- Contempt (green)

Right now these classes don't mean much (yet) outside of certain buffs

Each hero controls 2 classes, and any card belonging to those classes.\
Each card belongs to 1 class
